1. WHY P0D-A EXISTS
============================================================

P0B/P0C establish a source-specific attribute-transfer defect and a very large matched
native-contactability disparity.

The Best Bias Discovery rubric also requires IMPACT:
who is affected and why the disparity matters for emergency dispatch, evacuation routing,
disaster relief, or climate resilience.

P0D-A therefore asks a narrower question:

Does the frozen ATP phone-loss cohort include enough prospectively defined operationally
relevant service roles — medical contact, emergency/public response, and essential disaster
supplies — to support a concrete impact argument?

P0D-A does NOT:
- invent new categories after seeing counts;
- claim realized emergency harm;
- claim every phone is current;
- claim a missing phone makes a destination unreachable;
- use SVI to rescue impact;
- change the P0B or P0C effect.

============================================================
3. OPERATIONAL-IMPACT BASIS
============================================================

FEMA/Ready.gov preparedness materials identify:
- water and non-perishable food;
- prescription medications and medical supplies;
- fuel for vehicles/generators;
- emergency contact / communication information;
as concrete preparedness needs.

Therefore phone/contact representation for places serving these roles can matter to
preparedness and disaster logistics.

Permitted language:
"Native phone loss can reduce the map's ability to expose a direct contact channel for
operationally relevant destinations such as pharmacies, medical providers, food/fuel/water
suppliers, and emergency/public-service locations."

Not permitted:
- "People could not obtain medicine/food/fuel because Overture dropped phones."
- "Emergency responders could not contact these places."
- "This caused deaths/harm."
- "Every listed phone is current/correct."
