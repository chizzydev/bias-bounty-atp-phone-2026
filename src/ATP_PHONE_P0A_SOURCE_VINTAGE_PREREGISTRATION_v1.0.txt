  "AllThePlaces missing phone numbers".

============================================================
3. P0A SOURCE-LINK SUPPORT GATE
============================================================

Before any P0B phone inspection is authorized, require:

- >= 60 distinct exact ATP source records;
- >= 10 distinct scored tracts;
- >= 2 distinct challenge regions;
- multi-region / multi-tract membership ambiguity <= 2% of candidate source records;
- a deterministic frozen witness sample of up to 120 exact source-record/GERS pairs.

The witness sample is selected ONLY by ascending SHA256 of:
ATP_PHONE_P0A_v1|record_id|gers_id

No phone field participates in selection.

============================================================
4. AUGUST BRIDGE CONSISTENCY GATE
============================================================

Using the official August 2026 bridge files:
s3://overturemaps-us-west-2/bridgefiles/2026-08-19.0/

For the deterministic witness sample, require 100% exact `(record_id, GERS ID)` recovery
from bridge rows whose dataset is AllThePlaces.

The August bridge partition changed to provider-first paths. The runner may use the provider
recorded in the source struct when available; otherwise it may glob provider=* and filter
dataset=AllThePlaces.

A bridge mismatch is METHOD_UNRESOLVED.
Do not replace a source identity with fuzzy matching.
